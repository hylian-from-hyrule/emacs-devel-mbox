I'm not very familiar with `speedbar.el`, but based on a quick look at
`speedbar-reconfigure-keymaps` I think we have two options:

- Write the bindings "by hand" in the docstring (along with explanation
  about when which binding can be used) instead of relying on
  `\{speedbar-mode-map}`.
  That's upfront work and more maintenance work to keep it up-to-date
  when the code is changed.

- Try and get rid of `speedbar-reconfigure-keymaps`, maybe by relying on
  `:filter` to "statically" describe the dynamic part of the keymap and by creating
  two separate derived modes (one for the "frame" case and another for
  the "window" case).

to compute `localmap`.  Would the patch below be correct, or are there
cases where the buffer of the frame we end up selecting is not
`speedbar-buffer` (or do we really want to run
`dframe-after-select-attached-frame-hook` here)?

reviews you write is read by people who learn from them.
